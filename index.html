Our story

Vastra means clothing. Dev means craft of making.

VASTRADEV started with a simple frustration: most T-shirts are either thin and disposable, or expensive for the label alone. We wanted a tee that feels substantial, fits properly and keeps its shape after a hundred washes.

So we focus on a short list of things done well: the weight of the cotton, the cut of the shoulder, the finish of the collar, and graphics that are printed to last rather than to shout.

Fabric & fit

Built around the details

01

Heavyweight combed cotton

Combing removes short fibres, so the yarn is smoother, stronger and softer. At 240 GSM the tee hangs cleanly instead of clinging.

02

Pre-shrunk, garment washed

Every piece is washed before it reaches you, which softens the hand-feel and keeps shrinkage to a minimum.

03

Reinforced collar & seams

A ribbed, double-stitched collar and taped shoulder seams help the neckline hold its shape over time.

04

Soft-hand screen prints

Water-based inks sit into the fabric instead of on top of it, so graphics stay flexible and don't crack.

Questions

Good to know

How do I place an order?

Add your pieces to the cart, choose "Place order", and send us the pre-filled order message. Our team confirms availability, shipping and payment details with you directly.

How long does delivery take?

Orders usually ship within 2 to 3 working days and arrive in 4 to 7 working days across India, depending on your location.

What is your exchange policy?

If the size isn't right, message us within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange an exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed and with tags attached.

How should I wash my tee?

Machine wash cold with similar colours, inside out. Do not bleach. Tumble dry low or line dry in shade to protect the print.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet. International shipping is something we're working on. Write to us and we'll let you know when it opens.
